Joined as a Trainee Chemist in the quality-control department and was promoted to Assistant Manager QC in June 2019. Responsibilities documented in the CV include:

  • Supervising limestone, clay, sand and iron-oxide quality against specifications and permitted major/minor oxide limits.
  • Preparing raw-material samples from piles and quarries for chemical testing.
  • Conducting chemical analysis of raw materials, clinker and cement using conventional wet methods in line with ASTM standards.
  • Supporting raw-mix design and preparing solutions for gravimetric and analytical tests.
  • Performing proximate and ultimate coal analysis according to ASTM methods.
  • Preparing quality-control reports and documents.
  • Monitoring process areas including piles, raw mill, kiln feed, cement mill and plant operations.
  • Working with OPC Grade 53 and Grade 43 quality control.
  • Calibrating and operating laboratory instruments and using XRF machines and titration for raw-meal quality control.
